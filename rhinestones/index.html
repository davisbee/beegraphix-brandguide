03 · Artwork

Art That Sparkles

Rhinestone art is converted into a stone template, so bold, simple shapes work best. Detail finer than one stone disappears.

RequirementStandard
FilesVector preferred: AI, EPS, PDF, CDR. Raster at 300 dpi at final size is accepted and redrawn
Positive spaceLines and shapes at least 1.5 mm wide (wider than the stone)
Negative spaceGaps at least 0.5 mm
Minimum text height1" for outlined letters in SS10; 1.5" for filled letters
ColorsOne stone color per area, named from the stone color chart
ProofStone layout proof before production

Won't Reproduce

  • Photos, gradients and fine shading
  • Thin scripts and small text under the minimums above
  • Detail smaller than one stone

04 · Restrictions

Know the Fabric

Garment or conditionRule in Brandfora
Cotton, polyester, blends, nylonAllowed.
SilkNot offered.
Specially coated fabricsApproval only; test first.
Heavily textured fabrics (fleece pile, waffle knit)Approval only. Stones may not seat evenly.
Zippers, buttons, snaps, seams, pocketsTransfer stays clear; the press needs a flat surface.
Children's garments (toddler and infant)Not offered. Stones can come loose and are a choking hazard.
Designs over the max sizeArt review to resize.

Care: wash inside out on cold, gentle cycle; hang dry or tumble dry low.
