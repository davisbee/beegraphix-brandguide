02 · Color

Gold and Black

Gold is the spark. Hive black does the heavy lifting. White gives it room. Click any value to copy it.

Black on Gold
Text on gold is always black.

Hive #231F20 on gold passes contrast at 8.7:1. White on gold does not.

Gold on Black
Gold text only on black.

Gold type on white is too faint to read (1.9:1). On light pages, use gold as a bar, shape or underline instead.

Gold as a Highlight
On white, gold underlines.

Keep the words black and let gold sit behind them.

04 · Logo Rules

Give It Room

Clear space on every side equals the height of the "B" in the wordmark. Nothing goes inside that space.

Clear space

Minimum Sizes

  • Horizontal logo: 120px on screen, 1.25 in. in print
  • Stacked logo: 72px on screen, 0.75 in. in print
  • Hex mark: 16px on screen, 0.25 in. in print

Don't

  • Stretch, skew, rotate or outline the logo
  • Recolor it outside gold, black and white
  • Swap the colors of "Bee" and "Graphix," or set both in gold
  • Put the gold two-tone logo on a gold background (use the black version)
  • Rearrange the hex mark and wordmark into a new lockup
  • Add shadows, glows, bevels or gradients
  • Place it on a busy photo without a solid black band behind it
  • Retype "BeeGraphix" in any font

05 · Name & Voice

How We Sound

Like the coach who returns your call the same day. Direct, specific, a little fired up.

  • Write it BeeGraphix: one word, capital B and G
  • Short sentences, mixed with a longer one that carries the detail
  • Real numbers over slogans: "85 participants raised $25,016"
  • Talk to one person: your team, your store, your gear
  • "Bee Graphix," "Beegraphix" or "BG"
  • Corporate filler: solutions, leverage, best-in-class
  • Em dashes. Use a period, comma or colon instead
  • Generic claims you can't back with a number
