02 · Artwork

Art That Cuts

Glitter flake is cut on a plotter and weeded by hand, so art must be clean vector shapes in solid colors. Glitter is thicker than standard vinyl, so lines and gaps need more room.

RequirementStandard
FilesVector only: AI, EPS, PDF, SVG, CDR, fonts converted to outlines. Raster art is redrawn in art review
ColorsUp to 2 glitter colors per design, pieced side by side or layered glitter on glitter
Minimum line width0.07" (1.8 mm); 1/16" is the absolute floor
Minimum gap0.06" (1.5 mm) between cut pieces or inside letters
Minimum text height0.25" (6.5 mm), bold sans serif
FontsBold block and athletic faces; no thin scripts or fine serifs
ProofDigital proof showing size, placement and glitter colors

Won't Reproduce

  • Gradients, photos, halftones and shading
  • More than 2 glitter colors
  • Thin scripts, hairlines and tiny text under the minimums above
  • Detailed logos with many small interior cutouts (they tear when weeded)

These route to art review for a simplified version, or to BGX Print (DTF) instead.

03 · Restrictions

Know the Fabric

Garment or conditionRule in Brandfora
Cotton, poly/cotton blends, polyester, spandexAllowed. Polyester presses at a lower temperature.
Nylon, waterproof shells, coated fabricsApproval only. Heat can scorch or melt the fabric.
Heavily textured fabrics (fleece pile, waffle knit, mesh)Approval only. The vinyl may not bond fully.
Zippers, buttons, snaps, seams, pocketsTransfer stays clear; the press needs a flat surface.
CapsFront, left panel, visor and bucket hat only, on a cap press.
Designs over the max sizeArt review to resize.

Press settings (Stahls'): cotton and blends 302°F, polyester and spandex 290°F, 10 to 12 seconds, medium pressure, hot peel. Light glitter colors can look iridescent on dark garments; proof them on the actual garment color. Care: machine wash inside out, mild detergent, no bleach.

05 · Status

Where It Stands

  • Glitter brand: Stahls' CAD-CUT Glitter Flake.
  • Down sleeve (LSD / RSD) kept.
  • Cap left front panel code confirmed as CLP.
  • Cap sides (CL / CR) and cap back (CBF, CBA) removed from glitter flake, per Stefen.
  • Nike visors: 1.25" max height confirmed.
  • Stock glitter colors for the store picker.
  • Cutter model and roll width, to confirm the max sizes.
  • Glitter names and numbers: offer them, or always route personalization to BGX Print?
  • Set price tiers by size, colors and quantity.
