03 · Artwork

Art That Prints

Every ink color is its own screen, so artwork is built in spot colors: one PMS per color, up to 6 per location.

RequirementStandard
Preferred filesVector: AI, EPS, PDF, SVG, CDR, with fonts converted to outlines
Raster files300 dpi at final print size; routes to art review for separation
ColorsSpot colors called out by PMS (Solid Coated) or catalog name
Max ink colors6 per location
Minimum line width0.012" (0.3 mm); 0.02" recommended
Minimum negative space0.04" (1 mm) for gaps and knocked-out lines, or they fill in
Minimum text height0.14" (about 10 pt)
Halftones65 LPI at 45 degrees; gradients and shading print as halftones
Dark garmentsLight inks print over a white underbase, which counts as one of the 6 colors
ProofDigital proof showing size, placement and ink colors

Won't Reproduce

These route to art review for a simplified version, or to DTF instead.

  • Full color photographs (we print plastisol spot colors only, no simulated process)
  • Gradients without a halftone
  • More than 6 colors in one location
  • Lines or gaps under the minimums above

04 · Restrictions

Know the Fabric

Each one is a rule on the product: the location is hidden, capped smaller, or flagged for approval.

Garment or conditionRule in Brandfora
Zippers, buttons, pockets, seamsPrint stays clear. Full zip garments hide FF and use LC, RC or FB.
Pullover hoodiesFront uses FFH (14" x 10" max) to clear the pouch pocket.
100% polyester and performance wearApproval only. Dyes can bleed into plastisol; needs a low-cure or bleed-blocking ink.
Nylon jackets and bagsApproval only. Needs a nylon additive in the ink.
Reflective tape garmentsBack print uses FBR (9" x 10") to stay off the tape.
Heavily textured fabrics (fleece pile, waffle knit)Approval only. Fine detail breaks up.

06 · Status & Sources

Where It Stands

Setup progress for the Brandfora build, and where the numbers come from.

Setup Checklist

  • Setup fees: none, built into the price.
  • Production time: 7 to 12 business days, longer depending on the time of year.
  • White underbase counts toward the 6 colors.
  • Upper back yoke: 3.5" max.
  • Center chest (CC) and rally towel (TWL) added; ladies back, back side, side seam, women's chest, inside tag, rear end, bag pocket and onesie removed.
  • Personalization: added with DTF, not screen printed.
  • Custom PMS: extra charge, or same price as catalog inks?
  • Confirm halftone line count with our print partner (65 LPI assumed).
  • Approve the generic names for the 7 label-only inks (Emerald Green, Texas Orange, Cardinal Red, Athletic Gold, Dark Old Gold, Scarlet, Bronze Gold).
  • Set price tiers by color count and quantity (Davis).
  • Spine (SP): confirm the max size. 3.5" x 14" is borrowed from BGX Print. Flagged
  • Shorts leg, tall (LST / RST): markup says "2 x 6". Confirm whether that is the max or the default. Flagged
  • Vegas Gold: 467 C is the wrong PMS. Confirm the correct number. Flagged
