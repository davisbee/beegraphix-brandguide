Where It Prints

Designs print at the standard size unless the customer asks for a larger size, up to the max. 02 · Artwork

Art That Prints

DTG prints full color with no color count limit and a soft hand, best for photos and detailed art on cotton. On dark garments the printer lays a white underbase first, so art is built the same way as DTF: solid pixels, transparent background.

RequirementStandard
Preferred filesTransparent PNG at 300 dpi; AI, SVG, EPS and PDF also accepted
Resolution300 dpi at final print size (a 12" x 15" print needs 3,600 x 4,500 pixels). 150 dpi is the floor
ColorsRGB, kept inside the CMYK gamut. Neon and exact PMS matches are not possible
BackgroundTruly transparent. On dark garments, black areas meant to show the shirt should be transparent, not printed black
Opacity100% opacity on every element. Low opacity and transparent gradients print blotchy; use a halftone instead
Minimum line width0.02" (0.5 mm)
Minimum text height0.14" (about 10 pt)
ProofDigital proof showing size, placement and colors

Won't Reproduce

  • Neon, fluorescent and metallic colors
  • Exact PMS matches (screen print is the route when color must match a PMS)
  • Semi-transparent effects: glows, feathered edges, soft shadows
  • Lines or text under the minimums above

Rules

  • Name: max 14 characters.
  • Number: max 2 digits.
  • Color: one solid color per name or number, from the stock colors.
  • Fonts: Athletic Block, Block, Brody, Script and Brush Script, the same five as embroidery and BGX Print.

05 · Restrictions

Know the Fabric

DTG ink bonds to cotton fibers, so the garment's cotton content decides whether a product can offer DTG at all. Each rule below becomes a product rule in Brandfora.

Garment or conditionRule in Brandfora
100% cottonAllowed. Best color and wash life.
60% cotton or more (blends, tri-blends)Allowed. Colors print slightly softer on heathers.
Under 60% cotton, 100% polyester, performance wearDTG hidden. Route to BGX Print (DTF).
Nylon, waterproof shells, coated fabricsDTG hidden. Route to BGX Print or embroidery.
Heavily textured fabrics (fleece pile, waffle knit, mesh)Approval only. Fine detail breaks up.
Dark garmentsAllowed with pretreatment and a white underbase. Pretreat can leave a faint square that washes out.
Zippers, buttons, snaps, seams, pocketsPrint stays clear; the platen needs a flat surface. Full zip garments hide FF and use LC, RC or FB.
Caps, bags other than cotton totes, pantsNot offered in DTG. Route to BGX Print or embroidery.
Designs over the max sizeArt review to resize. Never stretched or cropped without approval.
